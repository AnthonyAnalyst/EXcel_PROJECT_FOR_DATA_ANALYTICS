# 📊 Market & Seasonal Sales Dashboard


## 🔹 Overview

This project provides an **Excel-based dashboard** to analyze **seasonal and regional sales trends**, offering actionable insights to optimize marketing, inventory, and operations.  

It highlights **peak months, top-performing regions, revenue growth**, and customer behavior across age groups.

---

## 🔹 Business Problem

Without data insights, AReL struggled to:

- Forecast sales trends by season  
- Identify top markets  
- Allocate marketing and inventory resources efficiently  

This dashboard addresses these gaps with **data-driven visibility**.

---

## 🔹 Objectives

- Identify **peak sales months and seasons**  
- Analyze **regional performance**  
- Track **monthly/yearly revenue trends**  
- Support **operational and marketing planning**

---

## 🔹 Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle Global Sales Dataset  
- **Format:** Excel Workbook with Pivot Tables & Charts  
- **Fields:** Date, Country/Region, Revenue, Quantity, Customer Age Group  
- **Cleaning:** Missing/duplicate data handled; standardized dates and regions  

---

## 🔹 Key Insights

- **Peak Month:** December ($9M+ revenue)  
- **Top Region:** United States ($27M+ revenue)  
- **Peak Day:** 24th ($3.4M revenue)  
- **High Growth Age Group:** 25–34  
- **Trend:** Sales increase from Q2 → Q4, strongly seasonal  

> **Insight:** Marketing campaigns should align with pre-peak months; inventory planning should match seasonal demand.

---

## 🔹 Recommendations

- Target **seasonal marketing campaigns**  
- Increase **inventory** in top-performing regions ahead of peak months  
- Monitor **monthly KPIs** to adjust strategy  
- Explore **growth in underperforming regions**  
- Implement **data-driven forecasting**  

---

## 🔹 Future Improvements
